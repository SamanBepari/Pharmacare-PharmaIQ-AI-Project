# Salbutamol 100 mcg Inhaler — Product Information (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE leaflet for demonstration. Not a substitute for the manufacturer's approved prescribing information.

## What it is used for
Salbutamol is a reliever inhaler that opens the airways. It is used to relieve the symptoms of asthma and other conditions that cause wheezing and breathlessness.

## Warnings and precautions
Shake the inhaler well before use and follow the technique shown by the pharmacist. If the reliever is needed more often than usual, or does not give relief, the person should see a doctor. Take care in people with heart conditions, high blood pressure or an overactive thyroid.

## Possible side effects
Tremor (shaking hands), headache, fast heartbeat and palpitations. Rarely, sudden wheezing after use or an allergic reaction occurs; seek urgent help.

## Storage
Store below 30 degrees Celsius, protected from frost and direct sunlight. The canister is pressurised: do not puncture, break or burn it, even when empty.

# Paracetamol 500 mg Tablets — Product Information (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE leaflet for demonstration. Not a substitute for the manufacturer's approved prescribing information.

## What it is used for
Paracetamol is used for the relief of mild to moderate pain such as headache, toothache and period pain, and for reducing fever.

## Warnings and precautions
Do not take more than the maximum daily dose stated on the approved label. An overdose can cause serious liver damage, even if the person feels well. Do not take other products that contain paracetamol at the same time. Take care in people with liver disease or who drink alcohol regularly. Ask the pharmacist before use in children, in pregnancy or with other medicines.

## Possible side effects
Side effects are uncommon when used as directed. Rarely, skin rash, allergic reactions or blood disorders occur. Stop use and seek medical advice if a rash or swelling appears.

## Storage
Store below 25 degrees Celsius in the original packaging, in a dry place, out of the reach of children. Do not use after the expiry date.

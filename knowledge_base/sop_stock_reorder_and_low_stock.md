# SOP-04 Stock Control and Reordering (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE document for demonstration. Replace with your pharmacy's approved SOP.

## Low stock rule
A product is treated as low stock when its quantity is 10 units or fewer. The Alerts page lists all low-stock products. Fast-moving products may need a higher reorder level, set by the manager.

## Weekly stock review
Every Monday the inventory team reviews low-stock products, expiring products and the previous week's sales. Each product is checked against its average daily sales to estimate how many days of cover remain.

## Reordering
Order enough stock to cover about 30 days of expected sales, taking current stock into account. Do not over-order items that expire within 90 days of delivery. Orders above the approval limit need manager sign-off.

## Receiving stock
Check each delivery against the purchase order. Verify product name, batch number, expiry date and quantity. Reject products with less than six months of remaining shelf life unless agreed with the pharmacist. Update the inventory system the same day.

## Stock discrepancies
Report any difference between the system quantity and the physical count to the manager. Do not adjust quantities without a recorded reason.

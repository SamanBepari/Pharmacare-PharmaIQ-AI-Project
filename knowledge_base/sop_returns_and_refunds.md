# SOP-01 Returns and Refunds Policy (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE document for demonstration. Replace with your pharmacy's approved SOP.

## Scope
This procedure applies to all counter staff, pharmacists and managers handling customer returns.

## Medicines that can be returned
Dispensed medicines cannot be returned or resold once they have left the pharmacy, because their storage conditions after sale cannot be verified. The only exceptions are items dispensed in error by the pharmacy, items supplied damaged or defective, and products recalled by the manufacturer or regulator.

## Return policy for non-medicine items
Sealed, unopened non-medicine items such as bandages and vitamins may be returned within 14 days with the original receipt. A refund is issued to the original payment method. Opened items are not refundable unless faulty.

## Procedure for an accepted return
1. Verify the receipt or sale record in the Sales module.
2. Inspect the product and record the reason for the return.
3. Obtain approval from the pharmacist on duty for any medicine return.
4. Place returned medicines in the quarantine area. They must never be put back on the shelf.
5. Record the refund and notify the manager.

## Approval limits
Counter staff may approve refunds up to the limit set by the manager. Larger refunds and all medicine returns need pharmacist or manager approval.

# Amoxicillin 500 mg Capsules — Product Information (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE leaflet for demonstration. Not a substitute for the manufacturer's approved prescribing information.

## What it is used for
Amoxicillin is a penicillin-type antibiotic prescribed for bacterial infections such as respiratory tract, ear, urinary tract and skin infections. It does not work against viral infections such as colds and flu.

## Warnings and precautions
Do not use in people with a known allergy to penicillins or cephalosporins. Tell the pharmacist about any previous allergic reaction. Complete the full course prescribed, even if symptoms improve. Take care in people with kidney disease.

## Possible side effects
Common side effects are diarrhoea, nausea and skin rash. Seek urgent help for swelling of the face, difficulty breathing or severe rash, which may indicate a serious allergic reaction.

## Storage
Store capsules below 25 degrees Celsius in a dry place, in the original packaging, out of the reach of children.

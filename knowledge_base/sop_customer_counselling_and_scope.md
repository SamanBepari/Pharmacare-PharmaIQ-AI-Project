# SOP-06 Customer Counselling and Scope of Practice (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE document for demonstration. Replace with your pharmacy's approved SOP.

## Who may give advice
Only a registered pharmacist may give advice about which medicine a customer should take, how much to take, or whether a medicine is safe for a particular person. Counter staff must not give personal dosage advice or recommend treatments.

## What staff can do
Staff can read out printed label information, show the patient information leaflet, explain storage instructions and opening hours, and direct the customer to the pharmacist.

## Referral triggers
Always refer to the pharmacist when the customer is pregnant or breastfeeding, buying for a young child, taking several medicines, has a long-term condition, or describes severe or worsening symptoms. Emergency symptoms such as chest pain, difficulty breathing or severe allergic reaction require an immediate call to emergency services.

## Use of PharmaIQ
The assistant provides reference information from approved documents and inventory facts. It does not diagnose, recommend treatments or calculate personal doses. Its answers never replace the professional judgement of the pharmacist.

## Confidentiality
Do not discuss one customer's purchases or health information with another person. Handle prescriptions discreetly.

# Warfarin 5 mg Tablets — Product Information (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE leaflet for demonstration. Not a substitute for the manufacturer's approved prescribing information.

## What it is used for
Warfarin is an anticoagulant used to prevent and treat harmful blood clots, for example in atrial fibrillation or after a clot in the leg or lung.

## Warnings and precautions
The main risk is bleeding. Regular blood tests (INR) are required to monitor treatment, and the dose is set by the prescriber based on those results. Many medicines, herbal products and alcohol interact with warfarin. Keep the intake of foods rich in vitamin K, such as leafy green vegetables, consistent. Warfarin is a high-alert medicine.

## Possible side effects
Bleeding, bruising, nosebleeds, bleeding gums and blood in urine or stools. Seek urgent medical help for severe bleeding, black stools or a serious head injury.

## Storage
Store below 25 degrees Celsius, protected from light, in the original packaging, out of the reach of children.

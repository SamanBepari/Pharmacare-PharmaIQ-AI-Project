# SOP-02 Handling Expired, Damaged and Recalled Stock (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE document for demonstration. Replace with your pharmacy's approved SOP.

## Expiry monitoring
The system raises an expiry alert for every medicine that expires within 30 days. The pharmacist reviews the Alerts page at least once a week. The principle of first-expiry-first-out (FEFO) applies: stock with the earliest expiry date is placed at the front of the shelf and sold first.

## Expired medicines
Expired medicines must never be sold or dispensed. When a medicine passes its expiry date, remove it from the shelf immediately and move it to the clearly labelled quarantine bin. Record the product name, batch, quantity and expiry date in the disposal register.

## Disposal
Expired stock is disposed of through the licensed pharmaceutical waste contractor. Do not throw medicines in general waste or flush them down drains. Controlled substances need two authorised staff members to witness destruction. Keep disposal certificates for at least two years.

## Damaged stock
Damaged packaging, broken seals, leaking bottles and products exposed to wrong temperatures are treated like expired stock. Report to the pharmacist and quarantine the batch. Contact the supplier for credit where applicable.

## Recalls
When a manufacturer recall notice is received, the pharmacist identifies the affected batch numbers, removes the stock, records the quantities, and returns the stock as instructed in the recall notice.

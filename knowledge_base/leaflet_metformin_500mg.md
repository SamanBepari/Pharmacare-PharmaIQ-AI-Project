# Metformin 500 mg Tablets — Product Information (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE leaflet for demonstration. Not a substitute for the manufacturer's approved prescribing information.

## What it is used for
Metformin is used to control blood sugar in adults with type 2 diabetes, usually alongside diet and exercise.

## Warnings and precautions
Take with or after meals to reduce stomach upset. It should not be used in people with severe kidney impairment. Treatment may need to be paused before some scans that use contrast dye, and during serious illness with vomiting or dehydration. Alcohol excess increases the risk of a rare but serious condition called lactic acidosis.

## Possible side effects
Common effects include nausea, diarrhoea, stomach pain and a metallic taste, which often improve over time. Seek urgent medical help for unusual muscle pain, difficulty breathing or extreme tiredness.

## Storage
Store below 30 degrees Celsius in a dry place, in the original packaging, away from children.

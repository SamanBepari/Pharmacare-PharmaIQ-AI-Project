# SOP-05 Controlled and High-Alert Medicines (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE document for demonstration. Replace with your pharmacy's approved SOP.

## Controlled medicines
Controlled medicines are stored in a locked cabinet. Only the pharmacist on duty holds the key. Every receipt and supply is recorded in the controlled drugs register, and the balance is checked at the end of each day.

## High-alert medicines
High-alert medicines are those that carry a heightened risk of serious harm if used in error, for example insulin, warfarin, epinephrine and digoxin. They are stored separately with a distinct label. A second pharmacist or trained staff member performs an independent check before dispensing.

## Look-alike sound-alike products
Products with similar names or packaging must be shelved apart and flagged with a warning sticker to prevent selection errors.

## Emergency medicines
Epinephrine for anaphylaxis is kept in an easily accessible location, checked monthly for expiry, and replaced as soon as it is used or expires.

## Incidents
Any dispensing error or near miss must be reported to the pharmacist in charge the same day and recorded in the incident log.

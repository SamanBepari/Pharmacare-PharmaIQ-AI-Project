# SOP-03 Storage and Cold Chain Management (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE document for demonstration. Replace with your pharmacy's approved SOP.

## General storage
Store medicines in a clean, dry, well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight. Room-temperature products are kept between 15 and 25 degrees Celsius unless the label says otherwise. Do not store medicines on the floor or near heat sources.

## Refrigerated products
Refrigerated products such as insulin and some vaccines must be stored between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. Never freeze them. Place them in the dedicated pharmacy refrigerator, not a food refrigerator. Do not store stock in the refrigerator door.

## Temperature monitoring
Check and record the minimum and maximum refrigerator temperature twice a day. If a reading is outside 2 to 8 degrees Celsius, quarantine the stock, tell the pharmacist immediately and do not sell the affected products until the manufacturer has confirmed they are still usable.

## Power failure
Keep the refrigerator door closed during a power failure. If the outage lasts more than the validated hold time, transfer stock to the backup cold box with ice packs and inform the pharmacist.

## Deliveries
Cold chain deliveries must be unpacked and refrigerated immediately on arrival and the temperature indicator checked and recorded.

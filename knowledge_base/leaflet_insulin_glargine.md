# Insulin Glargine 100 U/ml Injection — Product Information (SAMPLE)

> SAMPLE leaflet for demonstration. Not a substitute for the manufacturer's approved prescribing information.

## What it is used for
Insulin glargine is a long-acting insulin used to improve blood sugar control in people with diabetes. The dose is set individually by the prescriber.

## Warnings and precautions
Low blood sugar (hypoglycaemia) is the most common risk. Symptoms include sweating, shaking, hunger, confusion and headache. Never share pens or needles. Do not change the brand or type of insulin without medical advice. Insulin is a high-alert medicine and needs an independent check before dispensing.

## Storage
Store unopened pens and vials in a refrigerator at 2 to 8 degrees Celsius. Do not freeze and do not use insulin that has been frozen. Keep away from the freezer compartment. A pen in use should not be refrigerated and can be kept at room temperature for up to 28 days. Protect from light and heat.

## Possible side effects
Hypoglycaemia, redness or swelling at the injection site, weight gain and allergic reactions. Rotate injection sites.
